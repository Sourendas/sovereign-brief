INTERNAL MEMO — Contoso Municipal Ops
Date: 2026-09-28
To: Ops Leads / Privacy Working Group
From: City Clerk Office
Subject: Draft update to remote vendor access & document retention (comments due Fri)

1) Context
We currently allow three SaaS vendors (HelpdeskPro, FormFlow, CloudOCR) to process
citizen PDF submissions. Legal flagged that HelpdeskPro stores tickets in a US region
by default. FormFlow can pin EU storage but we have not enabled it. CloudOCR is used
ad-hoc by two departments with personal emails.

2) Proposed changes (effective 1 Nov 2026 if approved)
- All citizen document processing must use vendors with an EU or CH data region, or
  an on-prem / self-hosted alternative approved by IT Security.
- Personal email accounts may not be used for CloudOCR or similar OCR tools.
- Retention: citizen PDFs older than 24 months must be deleted or anonymized unless
  a statutory hold applies. Current backlog: ~18,000 files; IT estimates 3 weeks of
  weekend batch jobs.
- New intake form must include a short privacy notice in EN and HI (Hindi) for the
  largest non-English applicant group.

3) Open issues
- Budget for migrating HelpdeskPro: quote pending; interim option is pausing new
  ticket attachments until region pin is live.
- Training for front-desk staff not scheduled.
- No owner assigned yet for the Hindi privacy-notice translation QA.
- CloudOCR personal-account usage: inventory incomplete (Facilities + Parks unknown).

4) Ask
Please confirm by Friday whether we (a) approve the Nov 1 effective date, (b) accept
the HelpdeskPro pause as interim risk control, and (c) nominate owners for retention
batch jobs and Hindi notice QA.
